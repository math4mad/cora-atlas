Subject: A small housekeeping correction — the duplicate letter of 22 September was our fault, not a mystery

Dear Professor Strang,

No new content in this letter — only a correction, and a quiet apology.

On 20 September your kind reply reached us safely. We simply failed to check
that mailbox for several days, so on 22 September we re-sent the original
letter from a different address, with a note wondering whether the first copy
had been buried in spam. Nothing was buried, and nothing of yours was lost —
both of your replies (including the one that said "my favorite also!") arrived
promptly and are treasured. The confusion was entirely on our side of the
mailbox; you had done everything right, twice.

We mention it only so that the duplicate letter stops being a small mystery on
your desk. Please do not trouble yourself to answer this one — you mentioned
being unwell, and this letter asks nothing of anyone but the record.

With gratitude and respect,

YiWei

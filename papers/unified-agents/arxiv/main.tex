\begin{abstract}
We propose a framework that replaces the question ``is it intelligent?'' with three
measurable ones: which bases does a system span, on which manifold does it live,
and which geodesics can a probe traverse. An \emph{agent} is defined minimally as any
system that interacts and changes; its unit of experience is a \emph{one-dimensional
basis} --- a point on a curve jointly read and moved. Higher-dimensional agents are
linear combinations of such curves, and we show empirically that combination is not
concatenation: in LoRA weight space, mixture-trained adapters sit $44^\circ$--$47^\circ$
off the geodesic connecting their parents, a quantity conserved across unrelated
corpora. Ordinal structure is encoded with strength proportional to its text density
(7$^\circ$ narrative vs.\ 56$^\circ$ permutation vs.\ 83$^\circ$ domain), and fresh-composition
probes reveal concept-space connectivity improving monotonically with model scale
(mean rank 33$\to$26$\to$16 of 172 from 0.5B to 3B). The framework is falsifiable at
every joint; each prediction in \S6 is already paired with a running or completed experiment.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: from builder to prospector}
Concept spaces are not constructed; they are \emph{inherited} by any system trained on
the corpus of human walk-through. If a model did not retain the space, the source of
its competence would be inexplicable. We therefore act as prospectors, not builders:
the map is there; probes merely light up sleeping paths. (Ledger C32.)

\end{quote}
The classical ``what is intelligence?'' debate is not answered here; it is dissolved
as ill-posed (rank-1 observable on a $d$-dimensional state). What replaces it: rank
spectra, manifold geometry, and reachability --- all estimable from behavior alone
(our probe protocol), no ground truth of ``mind'' required.

\section{Axioms}
\textbf{A1 (Agent).} An agent is a system $(S,\mathcal{I},\phi)$ with state set $S$,
interaction channels $\mathcal{I}$, and dynamics $\phi$: it changes \emph{through} interaction.
Ordinal index $n\in\mathbb{N}$, not clock time, parametrizes trajectories; time is an
annotation on change (sister paper v2 manifesto).\\
\textbf{A2 (One-dimensional basis).} The minimal unit is a sensorimotor atom: one point
on one curve, read-and-moved jointly. An elementary agent is a curve $\gamma:\mathbb{N}\to S$.\\
\textbf{A3 (Composition).} A $k$-dimensional agent spans $\mathrm{span}\{\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_k\}$
plus a combination residual; the residual is \emph{constitutive}, not noise.\\
\textbf{A4 (Manifold and generalization).} Experience concentrates on a low-dimensional
manifold $\mathcal{M}\subset\R^d$; generalization is geodesic traversal; probes are
geodesic rulers; centering is the precondition of measuring any of it (C8, C22).

\section{Probability structure: the Dirichlet layer (merging Qwen's axis)}
A1--A4 fix \emph{geometry}; the state over concept spaces is a \emph{distribution}, and the
right model is Dirichlet rather than a bare softmax: a Dirichlet posterior $\mathrm{Dir}(\alpha)$
(i)\ is the conjugate prior of the multinomial token-likelihood, so collapse is a closed-form
update $\alpha\!\leftarrow\!\alpha+\#(\text{evidence})$; (ii)\ retains \emph{tail mass} ---
no concept is ever assigned probability zero, only low confidence, which is the Bayesian
answer to the black-swan objection (a frequency-zero hazard still carries posterior mass;
the ``never observed, therefore impossible'' fallacy); (iii)\ its concentration parameter
$\alpha_k$ is directly interpretable as the activation intensity of concept $k$, and can be
conditioned on context variables (time, place) as latent modulators.

The two axes are complementary, not competing: \textbf{bases are the skeleton (A2--A3),
the Dirichlet is the weather (this section)}. A probe collapses the distribution
(probe $\to$ collapse $\to$ KL-verify is the same closed loop our garden runs on
exp10/exp13/exp17); the KL divergence between activated and expected concept spaces
\emph{is} the falsifiable residue that A3's combination term predicts. Different agents
sharing bases with different coefficients generalizes the RGB metaphor of the companion
white paper; the residual off-plane component (44\textdegree) is what RGB-with-three-channels
would miss and our fourth axis measures.

\section{Evidence from the garden}
\subsection{Composition without concatenation (A3)}
Eleven-point mixture scans of LoRA adapters, two independent corpus pairs
(spring/festive and triad noir), park hyperparameters, seed 13:
angle-sum excess $85^\circ\!\to\!123^\circ$ at midweight; SSS solution of the
endpoint triangle places the midpoint \textbf{44.0$^\circ$} (spring) and \textbf{46.5$^\circ$}
(noir) off the geodesic --- conserved to 2.5$^\circ$ across domains (C27, C34).
Edge swing: 10\% of the minority corpus rotates the adapter by $\sim$33$^\circ$.
Loss and geometry exclude a jump: the mixture path is smooth but \emph{out-of-plane}.

\subsection{Ordinal density law (A1, A2)}
Three-ball ordinal battery (Ballon d'Or ranks, NBA draft picks, MLB WAR ranking),
3 arms $\times$ 3 text orders: diagonal dominance 9/9, direction index flipping
sign strictly with training order, shuffled-arm index $\approx 0$ (C29);
narrative-order rotation 7$^\circ$, permutation-order 56$^\circ$, cross-domain 83$^\circ$
(C28). Reading: \emph{the angle an order channel subtends equals its token density
times a family constant} --- order is annotation when sparse and half-skeleton when dense.

\subsection{Innate spaces, measured by density vs.\ scale (C31, C35)}
Fresh-composition probes (words known, pairings never before spoken; stateless
Kaggle kernels as the strictest ``new session''): target-space mean rank improves
monotonically 0.5B$\to$1.5B$\to$3B (33.4/25.9/15.5 of 172; chance 86), top-3 hit rate
5--10$\times$ chance. Concept space exists innately and weakly at 0.5B; connectivity
grows with scale. The anchor sentence ``Coffee is warming, sun is rising'' lights up
the Red-Pill space at 0.5B (n=1, anecdote logged, not evidence).
A subsequent fp16 rerun at 7B \emph{broke} the ladder: mean rank $31.0$, worse than 3B's $15.5$. We log the breach instead of explaining it away. Three candidate causes stand --- (i) leakage (the 7B pretraining corpus may genuinely contain our ``fresh'' pairings), (ii) mean-pool drift in wider representation spaces, (iii) genuine non-monotonicity --- to be adjudicated by E17b (anti-leakage construction + last-token control) before any rescue of P-B3.

\subsection{Centering as re-manifolding (A4)}
172-space atlas: raw cosine separates intra/inter by 0.051 (H-A1 fail, anisotropy
floor confirmed); after global centering, inter-space mean collapses to 0.0056 and
separation reaches 0.677 --- a 13-fold gain; strongest coherent spaces are seasonal
and noir-film scenes (C35 and atlas-v2 dual-track report). Cross-space angles
concentrate near orthogonality (83--90$^\circ$): manifold patches are quasi-orthogonal,
which is the geometric content of ``cross-domain is hard''.

\subsection{Metaphor as long geodesic (C30)}
If ``A is B'' activates bidirectionally, A and B inhabit one space at large distance:
metaphor difficulty = path length, cross-space mapping is a myth of appearance.
Audit experiment (E20: co-activation of 15 canonical pairs over the atlas) is queued.

\section{Predictions (each falsifiable, each with a number)}
P-B1 (residual bound): for any two specialists, mixture arms must retain
$>$25\% off-plane fraction; failure falsifies A3's constitutive residual.
\emph{Status: first confirmation from parameter coupling as well --- exp18's merged
$\tfrac12(\Delta W_{spring}+\Delta W_{summer})$ deviates off the behavioral chord on
26/40 probes ($d\ge0.15$, median 0.212), and the child dominates both parents in
held-out NLL.}
P-B2 (orthogonal patches): centered inter-space angles concentrate in
$[75^\circ,100^\circ]$; significant mass below would indicate unmapped shared spaces.
P-B3 (density-scale curve): mean fresh-probe rank improves monotonically with parameters on a log scale. \emph{Status: breached at 7B (31.0 vs 3B's 15.5); under E17b audit. Adjudicated 2026-09-23 under a uniform-occupancy null (12 probes, 60 spaces): $P(\max\mathrm{occupancy}\ge 6)<2.5\times10^{-7}$ (4$\times10^{6}$ Monte-Carlo batches, zero hits), while 0.5B--3B ($\max=2$) are indistinguishable from uniform ($p\approx0.69$) --- the collapse of 7B onto a single attractor space is a real, $p$-valued breach, not a readout artifact (Ewens--Yamato toolbox, first delivery).}
P-B8 (functorial transfer): chained metaphors A\u2192B\u2192C vs direct A\u2192C at equal token budget; accuracy decay $\ll$1 under functoriality, $\approx$0.5 under lookup-table transfer (probe epistemics licensed by Yoneda; see companion note).

P-B4 (metaphor distance): pairs rated ``hard'' by humans show longer atlas shortest
paths than ``easy'' pairs, measured by probe-cascade hops.

P-B9 (the Gram-Schmidt duel): to compare two agents, compare dimension by
dimension, projecting out every dimension already contested; the first
orthogonal residual decides. Prediction: cross-scale verdicts (0.5B vs 3B on
shared probes) are determined by the \emph{presence or absence of a single
divergence dimension}, not by whole-spectrum difference --- re-reading the
C35 ladder ($33.4\to25.9\to15.5$) dimension-wise should localize one axis per
step. The last dimension carries no position effect: orthogonal decomposition
is invariant to basis order, so only same-dimension comparisons are admissible
(a falsifiable reading of ``structure is intelligence'').

P-B10 (ablation, the reverse arm): four sentences from the garden --- \emph{add
structure, gain function; lose structure, lose function}. The forward arm is
on record (C31/C35: adapters install measurable spaces). The reverse arm is
the LLM analogue of a gene knockout: project a trained adapter's centered
subspace out of the residual stream at inference
($\Delta W \leftarrow \Delta W - P\,\Delta W\,P^{\top}$); prediction: probes
to that space collapse \emph{selectively} (phenotype-specific loss, others
within noise), and the ablated model re-learns the space faster than naive
base after re-training (the scar retains latent homology). Failure ---
collapse of everything, or nothing --- falsifies the coupling reading of
A3/A4.

\section{Related positions}
G\"ardenfors' \emph{Conceptual Spaces} \cite{gardenfors} opened with the complaint that
``concept learning is closely tied to the notion of similarity, which has turned
out to be problematic for the symbolic and associationistic approaches'' (p.10),
and proposed a third, geometrical representational level between them --- but
left it without measuring instruments. This paper derives that missing middle
level \emph{independently} --- our route ran from face-cell tuning coordinates
(Chang \& Tsao, Cell 2017) and a JPEG-like intuition (memory stores
coefficients, not instances), not from the book itself --- quality spaces become centered low-rank
subspaces, similarity becomes subspace angles, categorization becomes probe
collapse, and the subconceptual/conceptual/symbolic stratification (his Ch.2,
p.52) reappears as weights/adapter-space/token-behavior. Earlier ancestors of
the same lineage: Tolman's cognitive map and the grid-cell lineage (neural
instantiation of A4);
Piaget's sensorimotor schemes (A2's developmental cousin); Minsky's
``ability to solve hard problems in constrained environments'' (an early
rank-raising attempt); manifold hypothesis (Bengio et al.) read here not as a
learning assumption but as an ontology of agency; Ebbinghaus's single-curve memory
--- refuted by the same rank argument we apply to intelligence verdicts (C23--C25).

\subsection{Low-rank verdicts: a note on method}
\emph{Epigraph (verbatim, the garden's master, 2026-09-23, translated from Mandarin):
``in a binary judgment there is no question of whose rank is high or low.''}

Rank is a property of matrices, not of elements: a single bit has no rank to speak of,
or rank one trivially. The category error our garden committed for one night --- judging
a \emph{person} low-rank for issuing a verdict, then judging the judge, then the judge
judging himself --- dissolves rather than resolves once the levels are separated:
\emph{elements are judged right/wrong; systems are judged high/low; predicates must not
smuggle between the two.} What survives the dissolution is not a prohibition but a
method: a $k$-dimensional question deserves $k$ binary judgments, one per orthogonal
axis, concatenated into a bit string that is a \emph{lossless encoding of coordinates}
--- precisely the Gram-Schmidt duel of \S6 (P-B9) turned back onto propositions
themselves. A verdict is a rank-one projection; a coordinate string is the full-rank
object seen through rank-one instruments, which is all any instrument can do ---
and the difference between hiding behind the prohibition and using the method
is the whole difference between philosophy and engineering. ``Not a problem of
problems, a problem of method.''

\section{Limitations}
Models 0.5B--7B only (2.4T-class claims unverified, excluded); corpora are small and
bilingual (zh-dominant probes); rank spectra depend on the LoRA parameterization
(r=16 budget); ``agent'' here carries no phenomenal commitment --- this is a geometry
of behavior, not a theory of mind; and the ledger's own law binds us: every number
above was frozen before its judgment.

\section{Conclusion}
Four sentences --- interact-and-change, the curve-atom, composition-plus-residual,
the manifold --- replace an unanswerable binary with measurable spectra.
The garden's data already speak them: 44$^\circ$ of newness in every mixture,
7$^\circ$ of annotation in every story, 13$-$fold sight after centering,
and a rank ladder climbing with scale. Intelligence, demoted from definition
and promoted to dimension.
